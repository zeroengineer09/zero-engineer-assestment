BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Examination instructions

  • Choose one option for each question. Unanswered questions receive no marks.
  • The countdown uses the server deadline and does not reset on refresh.
  • The examination opens in fullscreen when supported. Switching tabs, exiting fullscreen, or a detected split-screen/major window resize may trigger automatic submission.
  • Browsers cannot reliably detect every split-screen configuration. Fullscreen and focus controls discourage switching but are not a guaranteed kiosk mode.
  • Once submitted, an examination cannot be restarted or edited.
  • Results do not reveal correct answers or explanations.
